# Development roadmap

- **U0.1:** Interactive 3D city, deterministic state, procedural assets, camera, selection and foundational tests (this delivery).
- **U0.2:** Expanded procedural generation: configurable block/district allocation, more roof and facade variants, less regular river geometry.
- **U0.3:** Lightweight vehicles and pedestrians.
- **U0.4:** Construction and interaction.
- **U0.5:** Save and load with versioned state.
- **U0.6:** Scenario and simulation mechanisms.
- **U0.7:** Research experimentation tools.
- **U1.0:** Stable reusable research platform.
- **Future:** Project 42 municipal finance module, separated from the engine.

These are future directions, not implemented features or authorized additions to U0.1.
